How much bank balance do I need?

There is no fixed amount set by UK Visas and Immigration. The officer checks three things: that your funds cover the full trip, that they match your income, and that the money has been in your account long enough to be explained.

A large deposit shortly before applying is one of the most common refusal reasons. If someone else pays for your trip, include their statements and a letter explaining the relationship.

Common refusal reasons

Unexplained deposits

Funds that appear just before applying, with no source shown.

Weak ties to Pakistan

Not enough evidence of job, business, family or property to return to.

Inconsistent details

Salary, dates or employer on the form don't match the documents.

Unclear purpose

No travel plan, or a trip length that doesn't fit your leave or budget.
